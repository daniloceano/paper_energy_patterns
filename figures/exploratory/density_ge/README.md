# Density Diagrams with Mean Ge

This directory contains density diagrams showing the distribution of cyclones in Lorenz Phase Space with mean Ge (generation of eddy available potential energy) indicated by discrete colors.

## Methodology

Each diagram shows:
- **2D density distribution**: Binned cyclone occurrences in phase space
- **Mean Ge coloring**: Discrete color bins using cmocean balance colormap (divergent red/green)
- **Density contours**: Black contour lines showing concentration of cyclones
- **Quadrant percentages**: Occurrence percentage in each quadrant (relative to sample size)

### Phase Space Definitions

- **Mixed Phase Space**: Ck (x-axis) vs Ca (y-axis)
  - Shows conversion (Ck) vs generation (Ca) of available potential energy
  
- **Imports Phase Space**: BAe (x-axis) vs BKe (y-axis)
  - Shows boundary fluxes of available potential and kinetic energy

### Ge Binning

Mean Ge values are discretized into 6 bins:
- < -2 W/m²
- -2 to -1 W/m²
- -1 to 0 W/m²
- 0 to 1 W/m²
- 1 to 2 W/m²
- > 2 W/m²

## Technical Details

- **Bin size**: 50×50 grid
- **Minimum points per bin**: 3 (bins with fewer points are masked)
- **Data limits**: Automatically calculated from 1st-99th percentile with 10% padding
- **Contour smoothing**: Gaussian filter with σ=1.0
- **Contour levels**: 20%, 50%, 80% of maximum density
- **DPI**: 300 (publication quality)

## Script

Generated by: `scripts/exploratory/density_diagrams_with_ge.py`

## Usage

These diagrams help identify:
1. **Dominant energy regimes**: Where cyclones concentrate in phase space
2. **Energy generation patterns**: Mean Ge distribution across different regions
3. **Energy Pattern characteristics**: How EP1, EP2, EP3 differ in phase space
4. **Lifecycle evolution**: How distributions change through phases
5. **Quadrant preferences**: Which energy regimes are most/least common
