# Legacy EP Structure Figures

> ⚠️ **LEGACY/ARCHIVED** — These figures were generated by the EP1-vs-EP2 analysis that was superseded in April 2026.

## What This Contains

This directory preserves figures from the **legacy EP structure analysis** that compared EP1 and EP2 cyclones using climatology-based anomalies.

## Figure Types

### Total Field Composites (EP1 vs EP2)
- `composite_egr_*.png` — Eady Growth Rate
- `composite_pv200_*.png` — PV at 200 hPa
- `composite_pv850_*.png` — PV at 850 hPa
- `composite_advT850_*.png` — Temperature advection at 850 hPa
- `composite_moisture_flux_*.png` — Moisture flux divergence at 975 hPa
- `composite_slp_*.png` — Sea level pressure
- `composite_rk_criterion_*.png` — Rayleigh-Kuo criterion at 250 hPa
- `composite_ke_advection_*.png` — KE advection at 250 hPa
- `composite_afc_250_*.png` — AFC at 250 hPa
- `composite_btcr_*.png` — Barotropic critical region at 250 hPa

### Anomaly Composites (Climatology-Based)
- `composite_*_anom_*.png` — Departures from 1991-2020 monthly climatology

### Difference Figures (EP1 - EP2)
- `composite_*_diff_*.png` — EP1 minus EP2 differences

## Why This Was Superseded

The legacy analysis:
1. Only compared EP1 vs EP2 (excluded EP3, 62.7% of cyclones)
2. Used climatology-based anomalies

The new canonical analysis:
1. Includes EP1, EP2, EP3, and EPALL (total composite)
2. Defines anomalies as EPx - EPALL

## New Canonical Figures Location

Current figures are in `figures/ep_structure/`.
